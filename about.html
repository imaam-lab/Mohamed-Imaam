A little about my journey, the way I think and what I’m working towards.

Hello, I’m Imaam.

I’m a graphic designer, web designer and developer, and entrepreneur from Sri Lanka.

Over the past three-plus years, I’ve been developing my skills across graphic design and digital creativity. My work includes visual identities, marketing graphics, poster design and modern web experiences.

I’m also the founder of Weelen Studio and Desycode Graphics. Building these ventures has helped me learn about creative direction, communicating with clients, presenting services and turning ideas into practical offerings.

I believe good design is more than appearance. It should make information easier to understand, help a brand feel consistent and support a clear business goal.
